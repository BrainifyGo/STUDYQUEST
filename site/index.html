Step 1 of 6 · Adding fractions

A fraction shows how many parts of a whole we have.

Imagine a chocolate bar broken into 4 equal pieces. One piece is 1/4 of the bar.

If the chocolate bar has 4 equal pieces, how many pieces make the whole bar?

Yes — that is it. All 4 pieces together make one whole bar.
